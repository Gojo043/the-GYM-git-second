Our Story & Philosophy

Crafting more than just coffee — fostering community, craft, and sustainability in every cup.

Born From a Love of the Bean

Founded in 2021, Aura Coffee Co. started with a single vintage roaster and a dream: to bring specialty, micro-lot coffees directly from passionate growers to coffee enthusiasts.

We believe great coffee isn't rushed. From hand-selecting harvest lots in volcanic soil regions to profiling our roasts second-by-second, we honor the craft of coffee making.

Our Mission

"To elevate daily rituals through exceptional artisanal coffee, sustainable agricultural practices, and warm community spaces."

Our Core Pillars

01

Direct Farm Trade

100% of our green beans are directly sourced, paying up to 40% above fair-trade market rates to local farmers.

02

Artisan Roasting

We profile each bean origin individually to highlight its unique flavor terroir — floral, fruity, or chocolatey notes.

03

Zero Waste Goal

All our takeaway cups, lids, and bags are 100% biodegradable, and coffee grounds are donated to community gardens.
