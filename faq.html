Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our beans, roasting schedule, and cafe visits.

Q: How fresh is the coffee you serve and sell?
All our whole-bean bags are roasted in micro-batches twice every week. We stamp each bag with the exact roast date so you know you are brewing within the optimal 5-21 day flavor window.
Q: Do you offer non-dairy milk alternatives?
Yes! We offer barista-grade Oatly Oat milk, Almond milk, and Coconut milk at no extra charge for all espresso and latte orders.
Q: Can I purchase whole beans ground for my home setup?
Absolutely. We are happy to grind your bag in-store for French Press, Pour-Over (V60/Chemex), Aeropress, Moka Pot, or Home Espresso.
Q: How does the monthly coffee subscription work?
You select your roast preference (Light, Medium, or Dark) and bag count. We automatically roast and ship fresh beans to your doorstep on your preferred schedule with free delivery. You can pause or cancel anytime.
Q: Do you host private events or catering?
Yes! We provide full mobile barista carts for corporate events, weddings, and celebrations. Get in touch with us via our Contact page for a custom quote.
